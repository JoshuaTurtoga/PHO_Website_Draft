Overview

Welcome to FDMH

"Sa PHO, Una ang Serbisyo!"

Francisco Dagohoy Municipal Hospital (FDMH) is a DOH-Licensed Level I health facility committed to delivering accessible and quality healthcare services to the people of Inabanga and its neighboring municipalities in Bohol. Operated under the Bohol Provincial Health Office (PHO), FDMH provides a comprehensive range of inpatient and outpatient medical services, from general consultations and surgical operations to laboratory diagnostics and maternal care.

We Offer:

DOH-Licensed Level I Hospital Provides primary care including general medicine, pediatrics, OB-GYN, surgery, isolation facilities, maternity, secondary clinical laboratory, and 1st level X-ray.

Programs & Milestones

Hospital Major Activities

Key infrastructure developments and programs undertaken by FDMH to strengthen healthcare delivery.

Hospital Major Activities

Conversion of old Admin Building into Hospital Quarantine Facility.
Creation of a new Hospital COVID Isolation Facility.
Division of ward facility into respiratory and non-respiratory wards to prevent COVID-19 transmission.
Construction of Hospital Perimeter Fence and new Isolation Facility.
Renovation and expansion of the Dietary Facility.
Purchase of new medical equipment to improve diagnostic and treatment capabilities.
Full operation of the hospital's TB DOTS Center to cater to and treat TB patients.
Construction of covered parking area and guard house.
Construction of new water system to improve hospital utilities.
