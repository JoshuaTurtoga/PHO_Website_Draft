Overview

Welcome to TBGDH

"Sa PHO, Una ang Serbisyo!"

Teodoro B. Galagar District Hospital (TBGDH) is a primary-level health facility dedicated to serving the communities of southeastern Bohol. Operated under the Bohol Provincial Health Office (PHO), TBGDH is committed to delivering accessible, efficient, and compassionate healthcare to all residents of Jagna and its surrounding municipalities.

We Offer:

DOH-Licensed Level I Hospital Provides primary care including general medicine, pediatrics, OB-GYN, surgery, isolation facilities, maternity, secondary clinical laboratory, and 1st level X-ray.

Programs & Milestones

Activities & COVID19 Response

A look into our hospital programs, milestones, and pandemic response metrics.

Hospital Major Activities

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Remote Audit compliance.
Conduct of Basic Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (BEMONC) training.
Renovation of the CT Scan building to expand local diagnostic capabilities.
Construction of the Hospital Holding Area to optimize patient flow.
Construction of the canopy for the Outpatient Department (OPD) waiting area.
Regular conduct of Internal Quality Audits and Management Reviews.
Community outreach programs including Free Circumcision (Operation Tuli) and Mobile Blood Donation drives.

COVID-19 Response & Recognitions

Vigorous COVID-19 Vaccination Program, performing 231 shots.
Established COVID ER and COVID OB-ER facilities to ensure clinical separation.
14-bed capacity COVID ward with planned expansion to 22 beds.

Recognition Received

First Place - Mobile Blood Donation Drive Achievement Award.
