Who We Are

Mandate, Vision & Mission

Mandate

The PHO Mental Wellness Facility is committed to promoting, protecting, and enhancing the mental health and well-being of all Boholanos. We provide accessible, holistic, and quality services through prevention, intervention, education, and advocacy.

Vision

A community where mental health is valued, quality services are accessible to all, and individuals experiencing mental health conditions are treated with dignity, respect, and deep compassion.

Mission

To deliver client-centered responsive care, promote mental awareness and education to break societal stigma, empower Boholano families, and foster safe supportive spaces that encourage healing.

Services We Offer

🧠

Mental Health Assessment

An initial mental health screening and assessment to better understand your concerns and determine the most appropriate intervention, treatment, or referral.

💬

Psychological Counseling

A confidential and supportive service that helps individuals manage emotional, psychological, and personal concerns through professional guidance and evidence-based interventions.

💊

Psychiatric Services

Psychiatric assessment, diagnosis, treatment, medication management, and follow-up care provided by a licensed psychiatrist.

🏥

24/7 Mental Health Hotline

Round-the-clock crisis support for individuals experiencing emotional distress or mental health emergencies. Trained personnel provide immediate assistance, psychological first aid, crisis intervention, and referrals to appropriate services whenever needed.
