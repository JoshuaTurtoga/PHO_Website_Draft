Provincial Hospitals

Our Hospitals

Government-operated hospitals providing accessible, quality medical services in every district of Bohol.

DOH-Licensed Level II Hospital

Garcia Memorial Provincial Hospital (GMPH)

Located in San Jose, Talibon, Bohol. A provincial hospital providing comprehensive secondary-level care to the northern and eastern parts of the province.

DOH-Licensed Level I Hospitals

Catigbian District Hospital (CDH)

Located in Poblacion West, Catigbian, Bohol. A district hospital serving the municipality of Catigbian and neighboring towns.

Francisco Dagohoy Municipal Hospital (FDMH)

Located in Inabanga, Bohol. A primary-level hospital serving the municipality of Inabanga and neighboring areas.

Teodoro B. Galagar District Hospital (TBGDH)

Located in Poblacion, Jagna, Bohol. A district hospital serving Jagna and surrounding municipalities in the southeastern part of the province.

Cong. Simeon G. Toribio Memorial Hospital (CSGTMH)

Located in Poblacion Sur, Carmen, Bohol. A primary-level hospital providing care to Carmen and the central interior district.

Infirmaries

Candijay Community Hospital (CanCH)

Located in Poblacion, Candijay, Bohol. An infirmary hospital providing primary care to the eastern Bohol district.

Cong. Natalio P. Castillo Memorial Hospital (CNPCMH)

Located in Barangay Basac, Loon, Bohol. An infirmary hospital covering the healthcare needs of Loon and surrounding communities.

Clarin Community Hospital (ClaCH)

Located in Poblacion Norte, Clarin, Bohol. An infirmary hospital serving Clarin and neighboring coastal towns in northwestern Bohol.

Maribojoc Community Hospital (MCH)

Located in Barangay Bayacabac, Maribojoc, Bohol. An infirmary hospital providing basic health and medical services.

Pres. Carlos P. Garcia Municipal Hospital (PCPGMH)

Located in Poblacion, President Carlos P. Garcia (Pitogo), Bohol. An infirmary hospital on the island-municipality serving the local community.
